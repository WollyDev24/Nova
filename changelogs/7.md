### 🚀 Nova v1.1.1 — Fold Wallpaper Fixes & Dev Channel Updates

- **Fixed Pixel 10 & 11 Fold Reverse Playback**:
  - Resolved the orientation and scaling glitch where *Pixel 10 Fold* and *Pixel 11 Fold* live wallpapers appeared rotated sideways or letterboxed into a thin horizontal strip when locking the screen or using Always-On Display.
  - Reverse lock transitions now stay crisp, upright, and perfectly edge-to-edge center-cropped on all phone displays.

- **Smoother & More Reliable Engine Transitions**:
  - Re-architected the internal video playback pipeline to reuse a single continuous hardware decoder.
  - Completely eliminates video surface re-attachment stutters, dropped frames, and black-screen flashes when waking or locking your device.

- **New Update Channels (Stable & Dev Branch)**:
  - Added an **Update Channel** selector in Settings allowing you to switch between official **Stable** releases and bleeding-edge **Dev Branch** builds directly from GitHub.
  - The built-in updater seamlessly detects and installs the appropriate updates based on your chosen branch.

- **Automated Continuous Delivery**:
  - Added continuous development builds signed with the production release keystore, ensuring hassle-free in-app upgrades between channels without signature mismatch prompts.
