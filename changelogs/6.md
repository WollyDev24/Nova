### 🎬 Nova v1.1.0 — Super Wallpapers, Ultra-Light APK & Rock-Solid Playback

- **New Super Wallpaper Collections**:
  - **Pixel 11 Fold**: Added *Pine*, *Midnight*, and their *Dark* mode variants with fluid lock/unlock transitions and dynamic Samsung One UI palettes.
  - **Pixel 10 Fold**: Added *Green*, *Silver*, and their *Dark* mode editions featuring smooth animated liquid shapes.
- **70% Smaller App Size (Only ~15 MB)**:
  - Base download size reduced from ~50 MB to just 15 MB.
  - Wallpapers are now downloaded on-demand with live progress indicators and saved offline permanently, keeping your storage clean and downloads fast.
  - *ColorOS 17 Flow* remains bundled right inside the app so you always have a ready-to-use live wallpaper instantly.
- **Always-Alive Live Wallpaper**:
  - Fixed an issue where the wallpaper could stop moving or turn into a static picture after app updates or returning from the lock screen.
  - Wallpaper playback now automatically recovers and resumes smoothly every time you wake up or unlock your phone.
- **Seamless Live Previews**:
  - Fixed the wallpaper preview sheet so applying a new wallpaper immediately switches to the selected design without replaying previous selections.
  - Clean edge-to-edge swipe preview with matching start-frame covers.
- **Reverse Animation Fixes**:
  - Fixed reverse lock animations stretching or letterboxing incorrectly during screen lock.
  - Lock transitions now cleanly retain full-screen edge-to-edge cropping.
- **Dynamic Samsung One UI & Material You Theming**:
  - System accent colors and UI palettes automatically adapt to the vibrant colors of your active wallpaper without disturbing wallpaper playback.
- **Wallpaper Management Tools**:
  - Added built-in batch import (`add_wallpaper.sh`), removal (`remove_wallpaper.sh`), and compression (`compress_wallpapers.sh`) tools for easily adding and managing custom super wallpapers.
