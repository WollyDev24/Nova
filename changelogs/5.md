### 🔑 Nova v1.0.4 — Multi-Tier Google Sign-In & Samsung One UI Fallback

- **Seamless Google Sign-In Fallback**: Added native Google Play Services Account Chooser fallback (`GoogleSignInClient`) when Credential Manager encounters `NoCredentialException` on devices such as the Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra.
- **Explicit Button Flow (`GetSignInWithGoogleOption`)**: Upgraded Credential Manager request to use official button-tap authorization option rather than lightweight silent authentication.
- **Detailed Diagnostics**: Added clear error messaging with Google Play Services status codes (e.g. Status 10 / DEVELOPER_ERROR, Status 12500) if Google rejects authentication credentials.
- **Deterministic Token Authentication**: Unified Firebase credential exchange across both Credential Manager and Play Services ID tokens.
