### 📱 Nova v1.0.2 — Tablet & Large Screen Optimizations

- **Adaptive Multi-Column Grid**: Responsive layout for large screens like the Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra (scaling dynamically from 2 to 5 columns).
- **2x2 Collection Gallery**: Tablet-optimized collection grid replacing ultra-wide stretched horizontal banners.
- **Spacious Collection Sheet**: Enlarged modal sheet width up to 1080dp with 4-column wallpaper picker.
- **Weighted Slot Balancing**: Community cards maintain natural aspect ratios with graceful spacer padding on wide displays.
- **Centered Modal Sheets**: Responsive max-widths across Settings, Upload, Profile, and Moderation dialogs.
