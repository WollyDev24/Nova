### 🌟 Nova v1.0.0 — Initial Release

- **HyperOS & ColorOS Super Wallpapers**: High-fidelity lock-to-home transitions and super live wallpapers ported to all Android devices.
- **Dynamic Lock/Unlock Triggers**: Wake-up and unlock animation replay with smooth pause at the end frame for zero battery waste.
- **Offline Bundle**: Embedded high-efficiency OEM wallpaper videos and preview thumbnails.
- **Custom MP4 Video Import**: Import and set your own MP4 videos as live wallpapers.
- **Material 3 Expressive UI**: Dynamic color theming, floating pill navigation bar, and interactive full-screen previewer.
